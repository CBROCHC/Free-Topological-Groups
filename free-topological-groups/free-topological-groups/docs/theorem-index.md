## Chapter 5 — Conclusions (p. 61)

* Freedom is **not** inherited by arbitrary topological subgroups, but is recovered for open subgroups (Graev) and kernels onto discrete groups.
* Markov-style statements require *total disconnectedness*; the positive theorems are Graev-specific.
* Perspective: relevance to group-based post-quantum cryptography (Anshel–Anshel–Goldfeld).
